AI vs the dictionary

A series. Each entry takes one question a learner of Serbian would really ask, puts it to a general-purpose chatbot, and sets the answer beside what the printed dictionaries say.

    Where is the accent in Beograd?

    Asked by a learner of Serbian, ChatGPT gives the Croatian accent of Beograd and never mentions that Serbian has another. Both are correct, each in its own standard, and a dictionary should say so.
