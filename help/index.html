Purchases and subscriptions

I paid, but Plus isn't unlocked

Restore the purchase from inside the app:

  • iPhone and iPad: Settings → Purchases → Restore Purchases.
  • Android: the gear icon on the search screen → Purchases → Restore Purchases.

Restoring finds a purchase made with the same Apple Account or Google account that the phone is signed in with now. If it finds nothing, write to support@govoridictionaries.com with the date of the purchase — the receipt Apple or Google emailed you is the quickest way to sort it out.

How do I cancel or change a subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by the store you bought from:

  • iPhone and iPad: the Settings app → your name → Subscriptions → Govori.
  • Android: Google Play → your profile picture → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions, or play.google.com/store/account/subscriptions.

Cancel at least 24 hours before the next renewal. Plus stays on until the end of the period you've paid for. The one-time purchase is not a subscription: there is nothing to cancel.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple and Google, not by me, under their own rules: request one at reportaproblem.apple.com for the App Store, or through Google Play's refund page.

Does my purchase work on my other devices?

Same store account — yes. A purchase made with your Apple Account works on every iPhone and iPad signed in with it; one made with your Google account works on every Android device with that account. On a new device, use Restore Purchases.

Between iPhone and Android — no. Apple and Google don't share purchases with each other, so a purchase on one doesn't carry over to the other.

Between the dictionaries — no. The Serbian and the Montenegrin apps are separate apps in the stores, and each is bought on its own.

The store won't let me pay

In some countries, and with some store accounts, Google Play or the App Store can't sell apps. If that's your case, write to support@govoridictionaries.com and say which app and which phone — we'll find a way.

I have a promo code

Open the Plus screen (tap any grammar table) and choose Have a promo code?. Codes from partners take money off the one-time purchase or the first year of the yearly plan.

The apps

Which devices does Govori run on?
  • iPhone with iOS 16 or later — the Serbian app also runs on iPad.
  • Android phones and tablets with Android 8 or later.

There's no Mac or Windows app; on a computer, use the web dictionary.

On Android, the app says it's downloading the dictionary

On Android the dictionary arrives from Google Play separately, right after the app installs or updates — about 50 MB to download. It needs an internet connection once and around 160 MB of free space. After that it works offline.

If the app was installed from anywhere other than Google Play, the dictionary can't arrive: install Govori from Google Play.

What works without internet?

The whole dictionary: search, meanings, examples, every grammar table, favourites and history. The Serbian app also has the pronunciation of core words built in.

An internet connection is needed only to buy or restore Plus, to send a report, on Android for the first dictionary download and its updates, and for the web searches offered when the dictionary has no answer.

Will I lose my favourites if I reinstall?

Govori has no account and no cloud sync: favourites and history live only on your device, so deleting the app deletes them.

On iPhone and iPad you can save them first: Settings → My Words → Export as CSV.

Money

Why isn't it completely free?

A lot of it is, permanently: search, meanings in English and Russian, usage examples, favourites and history. No ads anywhere in it. Searches are reported anonymously — that's how the "people looked for this and found nothing" list gets built — but with no account, no login and no profile, what comes back is trends rather than anyone's reading history.

What's paid is the depth — the grammar tables. Those are the part that takes years: a paradigm isn't scraped, it's verified cell by cell against reference works, and thousands of them have been corrected by hand.

I have no investors, I don't sell data, and I refuse to sell your attention to an ad network. That leaves one honest option: the people who get the most out of the dictionary pay for it, and their money pays for the next ten thousand words.

How is it priced?

Everything is unlocked for 14 days after you install — the full app, no card, no account. After that, lookups, meanings and examples stay free forever, and the grammar tables become part of Govori Plus.

Plus comes in three shapes, and the app shows the current price in your own currency:

  • Pečenje — one payment, yours forever. The best value and the one I'd pick.
  • Sarma — a yearly subscription.
  • Kafa — monthly, cancel whenever you like.

The food names are decorative; every option always shows exactly what it bills and when. No manipulation, no nagging reminders, and no push to start a subscription — the kind you later forget to cancel. The dictionary is deliberately built so that the free version and the paid one are both a fully working tool. And one purchase covers everything that ships afterwards.

Why offer a subscription at all?

Honestly, I'd rather sell you the app once — that's why the one-time purchase leads the list: over any real span of time it's the best value.

But not everyone is sure they'll stay with Serbian for long, or that the dictionary will turn out to be that useful to them. Paying for "forever" up front makes little sense then: a month or a year costs considerably less than the one-time purchase, and it's an unhurried way to find out whether this is your tool. If it turns out to be, the one-time purchase isn't going anywhere.

Can I donate?

There's no tip jar. If you want to support the project, buy the one-time purchase — that is the donation, and it gets you the whole dictionary. If you already own it, telling one person who's learning the language does more for it than money.

Privacy

What do you collect, and how do I have it deleted?

There's no account and nothing to sign up for. The apps send anonymous usage and crash reports tied to a random installation ID, and whatever you write in a report. The privacy policy lists all of it.

To have your data deleted, write to support@govoridictionaries.com.

Reports and contact

How do I reach you?

Write to support@govoridictionaries.com, or ask in the Telegram group. I read both myself.

From inside the app:

  • A mistake in an entry: Report a problem on the word's card.
  • A word that's missing: report it from the screen that says nothing was found.
  • Anything else: Settings → Report a Problem.
